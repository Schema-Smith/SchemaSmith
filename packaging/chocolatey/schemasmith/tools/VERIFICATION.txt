VERIFYING THE EMBEDDED BINARIES
-------------------------------

The Windows binaries are Authenticode-signed via Azure Trusted Signing
with a Microsoft-issued certificate registered to "SchemaSmith
Contributors". You can verify the signature on your machine:

  signtool verify /pa /v "C:\ProgramData\chocolatey\lib\schemasmith\tools\schemaquench.exe"
  signtool verify /pa /v "C:\ProgramData\chocolatey\lib\schemasmith\tools\schematongs.exe"
  signtool verify /pa /v "C:\ProgramData\chocolatey\lib\schemasmith\tools\datatongs.exe"
  signtool verify /pa /v "C:\ProgramData\chocolatey\lib\schemasmith\tools\schemashears.exe"

Each command should report "Successfully verified" against a SchemaSmith
Trusted Signing certificate. (signtool ships with the Windows SDK; if it
is not on PATH, look under
"C:\Program Files (x86)\Windows Kits\10\bin\<version>\x64\".)
